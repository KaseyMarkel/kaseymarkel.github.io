      Light up your own species

      Paste a list: one species per line, scientific names work best (Monstera deliciosa). Common names, "Genus sp.", family names and CSV exports (eBird / Merlin, iNaturalist, spreadsheets with a "Scientific name" column) work too. Each species glows from its tip all the way down to the root.

      Everything runs in your browser. Names the tree doesn't recognise are looked up on GBIF (synonyms and common names). Your list lives only in the page link (copy it to share or come back later); nothing is saved on a server.

      Evo Tree Viewer

      A phylogeny of all land plants, drawn as a tree. Imagine a wire sculpture with one wire per species. Wires bundle together toward the root, so a branch's cross-sectional area grows linearly with the number of species it carries (that's also Leonardo's rule for real trees). Every leaf is one extant species.

      The glowing branches trace a species list back to the root. By default that's ~140 common food plants (switch to just the fruit, berries, or herbs & spices under Lists): notice how they bunch into a handful of families (grasses, legumes, the cabbage and rose families), and how few lineages outside the flowering plants feed us. Kasey's living wall is there too. Use ✦ Your species list to light up your own, 🌱 Only these species to grow a tree of just your list, or ◉ Your species only to fade the rest of the tree back.

      Data. Topology: Open Tree of Life synthetic tree, induced on ~14,500 genera; genera it lacks hang from their family's node. Species counts: World Flora Online Plant List (June 2026), accepted species only. (WFO also holds ~133,000 species names nobody has evaluated yet; some are real species, so the true count is somewhat higher.) Species inside a genus aren't resolved; they're drawn as a cluster of leaves at the genus twig. Branch lengths are artistic, not time.

      Controls. The tree sits on a turntable: drag to spin it and tilt it toward you · scroll or pinch to zoom (keep going to fly into the crown) · right-drag, shift-drag or two-finger drag to move up, down and sideways · double-tap a branch to put it at the centre of the turntable · ⌂ Reset to go back. Keys: ← → spin, ↑ ↓ up/down, W S zoom.
