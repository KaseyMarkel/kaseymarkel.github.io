      Evo Tree Viewer

      A phylogeny of all land plants, drawn as a tree. Imagine a wire sculpture with one wire per species. Wires bundle together toward the root, so a branch's cross-sectional area grows linearly with the number of species it carries (that's also Leonardo's rule for real trees). Every leaf is one extant species.

      The glowing branches trace the plants growing in my living wall back to the root.

      Data. Topology: Open Tree of Life synthetic tree, induced on ~14,500 genera; genera it lacks hang from their family's node. Species counts: World Flora Online Plant List (June 2026), accepted species only. Species inside a genus aren't resolved; they're drawn as a cluster of leaves at the genus twig. Branch lengths are artistic, not time.

      Controls. Drag to orbit · scroll or pinch to zoom · right-drag or two-finger drag to pan · tap a branch for details · double-tap to fly to it.
